The Harness Gen 3 ASE Strategy and plan: from aspiration to implementation, from prototype to production

The Harness is the platform we deploy into a council's own cloud to host, connect and grow the apps and synthetic workers that run their services. This plan sets out six initiatives, who owns each piece of work, and a working demonstration every fortnight from this week onwards.

Every initiative has a demo in the next two weeks. A rough version working on screen beats a polished slide.

Building compliance certificate validation runs through every initiative, so each step adds a link to one visible chain.

    Synthetic Workers

    What we mean by a synthetic worker, and the plan for bringing synthetic workers into council services through the Harness.

    Definition What we mean by a synthetic worker

    A synthetic worker is an AI agent set up like a role in the team. It has a job description, uses only the systems the Harness has wrapped for it, makes only the decisions it has been delegated, and hands every decision that matters to a named officer to confirm. It is measured the way the service measures its staff.

    • A job descriptionA defined role, set up like a member of the team.
    • Wrapped systems onlyWorks only through systems the Harness has wrapped for it.
    • Delegated decisionsMakes only the decisions it has been delegated.
    • A named officer confirmsEvery decision that matters goes to a named officer.
    • Measured like staffMeasured the way the service measures its staff.

    Estate Management

    Estate Management covers everything we need to know and do about a council's apps. We discover what they use, record it in the App Register, decide what happens to each app, and then wrap or rebuild it through the Harness. The SME engine writes into the register, and the Harness prints from it.
